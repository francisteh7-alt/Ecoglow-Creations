About EcoGlow

EcoGlow Creations is a student-led sustainability project focused on reuse, hygiene, and improving classroom life through creative recycling.

Home / About

Our Mission

EcoGlow Creations was born from real problems we face every day at Limkokwing University. Our classrooms lack tissue holders, plastic waste overflows, and learning spaces often feel bare.

We asked how one project could solve all three problems at once. By transforming campus waste into tissue holders, decorative products, and classroom accessories, we create value while improving the environment.

This is more than recycling. It is a practical model for sustainability, student innovation, and community improvement.

  • Campus Sustainability
  • Student Hygiene
  • Circular Economy
  • Better Learning Spaces

Our Vision

To become a leading campus-based recycling and product innovation initiative in Sierra Leone.

Practical Innovation

Create useful products from everyday waste materials.

Student Empowerment

Give students a chance to learn, lead, and build with purpose.

Scalable Impact

Develop a model that can be copied by other schools and campuses.

EcoGlow Creations
