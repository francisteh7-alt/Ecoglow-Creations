How We Transform Waste

From collection to delivery, every step is designed to be simple, sustainable, and useful for classrooms.

Home / Process

Our Production Journey

The process is built around teamwork, cleanliness, and creative reuse.

01

Collect

We gather bottle caps, plastic bottles, cartons, and other recyclable items from hostels, classrooms, cafeterias, and campus waste bins.

02

Clean & Sort

Materials are washed, sanitized, and sorted by type and color to prepare them for creative reuse and attractive finishing.

03

Design & Assemble

We arrange caps into patterns, fix them to carton bases, and refine the products so they are strong, neat, and practical.

04

Deliver to Classrooms

Finished products are delivered back to campus classrooms, libraries, and study spaces for real use.

What Happens Behind the Scenes

Sanitation First

Clean materials reduce odor, improve safety, and make the final product suitable for classroom use.

Creative Design

We use color patterns and simple layouts to make the recycled items look attractive and modern.

Local Delivery

Products are returned to the same environment where the materials were gathered, completing the circular cycle.

EcoGlow Creations

Turning bottle caps, cartons, and classroom waste into practical products that improve hygiene, beauty, and sustainability across Sierra Leone.
