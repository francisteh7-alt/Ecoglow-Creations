The Campus Problem

At Limkokwing University and schools across Sierra Leone, basic classroom needs, waste management, and learning environment quality still need practical solutions.

Home / Problem

Why EcoGlow Started

We identified everyday challenges that affect comfort, hygiene, and the appearance of learning spaces.

No Tissue Holders in Classrooms

Students and lecturers often rely on shared cloth or personal tissue because many classrooms do not have tissue holders. That makes hygiene harder to maintain.

Issue #1

Plastic Waste Overflow

Bottle caps, water bottles, and cartons are discarded every day. Without proper recycling habits, the waste piles up around campus and nearby areas.

Issue #2

Bare Learning Spaces

Many classrooms lack decorative, functional items. A more attractive space can improve motivation, concentration, and pride in the learning environment.

Issue #3

What the Problem Causes

Hygiene Challenges

Limited tissue access can affect cleanliness and comfort during lessons and group work.

Environmental Pressure

Uncollected waste increases pollution and reduces the quality of shared campus spaces.

Lower Morale

Learning in dull and untidy spaces can affect student enthusiasm and classroom pride.

Our Response

EcoGlow turns these challenges into opportunities by collecting waste and creating products that are useful, clean, and attractive.

Recycle

Collect and redirect plastic waste.

Build

Transform materials into classroom items.

Improve

Support hygiene and learning comfort.

Inspire

Encourage creativity and student action.
